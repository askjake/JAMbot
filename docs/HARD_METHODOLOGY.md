# HARD Methodology — Human Adjudication Request Dossier
## Generation, Scoping, and Processing

**Protocol ID:** `hard-methodology`
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-06-23
**Owner:** montjac
**Status:** ACTIVE

---

## 1. PURPOSE

A HARD (Human Adjudication Request Dossier) is the formal document used to request an independent human determination on a structured evidence case generated by the STB Field RCA system. Its purpose is narrow and precise: to get a qualified human reviewer to confirm or reject whether a specific frozen evidence packet genuinely represents a specific issue event on a specific receiver on a specific date.

A HARD is **not** a general review request, a bug report, or a log-sharing exercise. It is a provenance gate. The reviewer's determination, once validated against the packet and attestation contract, unlocks the case for consideration as validated learning or evaluation provenance. No case is promoted to claim-grade truth without a successfully processed HARD.

---

## 2. WHEN TO GENERATE A HARD

Generate a HARD when all of the following are true:

- A structured evidence case exists in the STB Field RCA casebook
- The case has an outcome status of `HUMAN_ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED` or `OUTCOME_NOT_HUMAN_CONFIRMED`
- An evidence bundle (`heb_*`) and a human adjudication packet (`hrp_*`) have been generated and are in `PACKET_VALID` / `CURRENT_AND_VALID` state
- A qualified human reviewer has been identified who has direct domain expertise for the issue profile in question
- The packet integrity status is `PASS` and `reviewer_ready: true`

Do **not** generate a HARD for:

- Packets with `integrity_status: FAIL` or unresolved blocking codes
- Cases where coverage is incomplete and the missing log families are material to the adjudication question — resolve coverage first or explicitly scope the HARD to acknowledge the limitation
- Cases where no qualified reviewer has been identified
- Multiple packets bundled together unless they are independently reviewable and the reviewer has capacity for all of them — prefer one packet per HARD

---

## 3. REVIEWER SELECTION

The reviewer must meet all of the following criteria:

- Has direct, documented expertise with the issue profile (e.g., personally investigated the root cause mechanism in a Jira ticket, wrote the relevant firmware code, or conducted field analysis of the same failure type)
- Is independent — has not contributed to generating or classifying the evidence being reviewed
- Is a human — AI-assisted or automated responses are explicitly prohibited by the attestation contract
- Has a corporate identity that can be verified via their Jira account and email

Document the reviewer qualification in the HARD's "Why [Reviewer]" section with a specific reference (Jira ticket, CCShare log path, or other traceable record). Do not use generic language like "has experience with STBs."

---

## 4. HARD SCOPE RULES

Each HARD must be scoped to exactly one independently reviewable unit. The preferred scope is **one packet per HARD**.

Multiple packets may be included in a single HARD only when:

- All packets share the same issue profile
- All packets are in `PACKET_VALID` / `CURRENT_AND_VALID` state
- All packets have complete coverage (no `⚠️ Incomplete` flags), or the incomplete ones are explicitly called out with a `NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE` guidance note
- The reviewer has explicitly agreed to review the full set

If any packet in a proposed multi-packet HARD has incomplete coverage or significantly different signature character from the others, split it into a separate HARD or defer it until coverage improves.

**Wording constraints:**

- Do not write "we need your sign-off before any of them can be used as ground truth in the benchmark." Use instead: "we need an independent human determination before this case can be considered for validated learning or evaluation provenance."
- Do not write "these responses will be recorded as immutable validation candidates." Use instead: "After you respond, the decision will be validated against the current packet and attestation contract. It will not be persisted or used to update case truth without a separate reviewed import step."
- The attestation statement must apply to the specific packet(s) in the HARD, not a generic set.

## 5. GENERATING A HARD

### Step 1 — Verify Packet Readiness

Call `read_human_adjudication_packet` for the target packet ID. Confirm:

- `integrity_status: PASS`
- `reviewer_ready: true`
- `current_state_blockers: []`
- `response_contract_available: true`
- `negative_conclusions_allowed` is set correctly for the coverage state

If any of these are not satisfied, resolve blockers before proceeding.

### Step 2 — Read the Evidence Bundle

Call `read_human_evidence_bundle` for the `evidence_bundle_id` on the packet. Review:

- The signature category breakdown (`direct_issue`, `supporting_context`, `known_false_positive`)
- Any `contradictions` or `limitations` in the bounded evidence
- Coverage completeness — note any missing log families

This step is required. The HARD's "What the Evidence Contains" section must reflect the actual bundle contents, not generic boilerplate.

### Step 3 — Identify the Reviewer

Identify the reviewer using the criteria in Section 3. Pull the relevant Jira ticket(s) that document their prior investigation. The "Why [Reviewer]" section must cite specific evidence of their qualification.

### Step 4 — Complete the HARD Template

Fill in the blank HARD template (`HARD_BLANK_TEMPLATE.md`) with:

- All packet metadata from Step 1
- Evidence characterization from Step 2
- Reviewer qualification from Step 3
- The core question scoped to the exact receiver, date, and issue profile
- Post-review processing notes specific to this packet

Do not leave any `[placeholder]` fields unfilled. Do not copy boilerplate from a prior HARD without verifying it applies to the current packet.

### Step 5 — Create the Jira Ticket

Create a Jira ticket in the appropriate project (typically `DATASO2` for RCA provenance work). The ticket must include:

- Summary: `[Human Adjudication] [issue_profile] — [Reviewer Name] — [RXID] / [event_date]`
- Assignee: the reviewer
- The completed HARD as the ticket description or a linked attachment
- The review package file (`REVIEW_PACKAGE_hrp_*.md`) attached directly to the ticket
- Links to the root-cause investigation ticket(s) that qualify the reviewer

### Step 6 — Send the Request Message

Send the reviewer a short message (Jira comment, Slack, or email) that:

- Points to the Jira ticket by URL
- States in one sentence why they are the right person for this
- States what the ask is (one decision, one packet)
- Does not summarize or paraphrase the evidence — direct them to the review package

## 6. PROCESSING A HARD RESPONSE

When the reviewer replies (typically as a Jira comment):

### Step 1 — Validate the Response Payload

Call `validate_human_adjudication_response_payload` with the reviewer's decision fields. Confirm:

- All required fields are present: `reviewer_name`, `reviewer_role`, `decision_timestamp`, `decision`, `rationale`, `evidence_items_reviewed`, `external_reference`, `authorship_type`
- The decision is from the allowed list
- The attestation acknowledgements are all `true`
- `authorship_type` is `human`
- `not_authored_by_ai_or_automation` is acknowledged

If any field is missing or the attestation is incomplete, go back to the reviewer for clarification before proceeding.

### Step 2 — Submit the Response

Call `submit_human_adjudication_response` with `dry_run: true` first. Review the preview. If clean, resubmit with `dry_run: false` and `confirm_submit` set.

### Step 3 — Resolve the Queue Item

Call `resolve_human_review_queue_item` with the `response_id` returned from Step 2. Set `record_validation_candidate: true`. Note the `candidate_id` returned.

### Step 4 — Grade Provenance

Call `grade_outcome_provenance` for the case. Confirm the provenance grade has advanced. Do not promote to claim-grade unless `pilot_grade` or higher is confirmed.

### Step 5 — Record the External Validation Candidate

Call `record_external_validation_candidate` with:

- `source_reference`: the reviewer's Jira comment URL
- `source_type`: `jira`
- `human_author`: reviewer's full name
- `human_author_role`: reviewer's role
- `statement_summary`: one-sentence summary of the decision and rationale
- `exact_event_linkage: true` if the reviewer referenced specific log lines or timestamps

### Step 6 — Update the Jira Ticket

Add a comment to the Jira ticket documenting:

- The response received
- The validation outcome
- The `candidate_id` and `response_id` for traceability
- Whether the case is now eligible for claim-grade promotion or requires follow-up

---

## 7. OUTCOME STATES AND FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS

| Reviewer Decision | Follow-up Action |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| `CONFIRM_ISSUE` | Grade provenance, record validation candidate, proceed toward claim-grade promotion |
| `CONFIRM_NO_ISSUE` | Record negative candidate, update case outcome, do not use as positive benchmark |
| `CONFIRM_GOOD_BASELINE` | Evaluate case for known-good control pool via `evaluate_baseline_candidate` |
| `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` | Document gap, do not promote, consider targeted log re-upload |
| `NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE` | Identify missing log families, plan Grasshopper upload, create follow-up HARD |
| `WRONG_RECEIVER_OR_EVENT` | Quarantine packet, investigate attribution error, do not promote |

---

## 8. CONSTRAINTS AND PROHIBITIONS

- **One packet per HARD** is the default. Multi-packet HARDs require explicit justification and reviewer agreement.
- **Do not bundle incomplete-coverage packets** with complete-coverage packets in the same HARD without explicit callouts and `NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE` guidance.
- **Do not paraphrase the attestation.** The canonical attestation statement must appear verbatim.
- **Do not mark outcomes as claim-grade** until `grade_outcome_provenance` returns `pilot_grade` or higher, regardless of how confident the reviewer's language is.
- **Do not accept AI-authored responses.** If there is any indication the reviewer's response was AI-generated, reject it and restart the HARD with a fresh request.
- **Do not reuse a prior HARD's wording** for a different packet without verifying that the evidence characterization, reviewer qualification, and core question are accurate for the new packet.
- **Do not skip the response validation step.** Partial or informal responses (e.g., "looks right to me") do not satisfy the attestation contract.
